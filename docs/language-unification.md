# Language Unification — Seven-Step Research on Whether It Matters

**R&D filing RND-21. Seven online research hops on language
unification, filed to answer one question: does it matter at all?
Answer: yes — but not as one language. The space's two-standard
decision (English + Chinese) is not preference; it is the
empirically strongest pair. Filed with the GOV-10 (Two-Tongue
Doctrine) as its research base.**

---

## Step 1 — The invented-language failure (Esperanto)

**The research:** Esperanto (Zamenhof, 1887) is the most successful
constructed language ever — and still failed as a universal
tongue. The numbers file why. Zamenhof asked people to sign a
promise to learn it once ten million promised. He received about a
thousand. Native speakers ("denaskuloj") number ~2,000. The PMC
article on Esperanto's decline files the fatal triad: no
mother-tongue speakers, no network to practice with, and not a
true standard. Unofficial words spread and drifted.

**Filed:** inventing a tongue fails on
network effect. A language with no speakers cannot recruit
speakers. Esperanto proves it. The space does not invent a language.

## Step 2 — The two-tier success (Singapore)

**The research:** Singapore (1965) chose FOUR official
languages — English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil — with a two-tier
model. English is the working language: commerce, science,
inter-ethnic communication. The mother tongues carry identity.
The policy was pragmatic, not patriotic: English for economic
survival, Mandarin/Malay/Tamil to hold ethnic identity. It is
widely credited with Singapore's economic rise and social
stability. The research files the caveat: less than 40% of
students reached minimum competency in two tongues. The
trend is toward English + one — a practical bilingualism.

**Filed:** two-tier works. A working language for
the machine and the market; identity languages for the people.
The space's two standards — English + Chinese — are the same pair
Singapore converged on.

## Step 3 — Chinese's global status (the honest read)

**The research:** Gil (2011), "A comparison of the global status
of English and Chinese": Chinese is FAR from a global language.
It is less entrenched as an official language worldwide. It has
fewer second-language speakers. But Mandarin is the most-spoken
first language on Earth (~1.1 billion). It is one of the UN's six
official languages. And it is strategically rising with China's
economic weight. "The Rise of Chinese as a Global Language"
(ResearchGate) files the same arc: strategic role in economics
and education, real obstacles to global lingua-franca status.

**Filed:** Chinese is not yet English's rival as a
global lingua franca. But the space does not need a global
lingua franca. It needs a second standard with depth, history,
and the largest L1 population on Earth. Chinese is that.

## Step 4 — The AI/code reality (the decisive hop)

**The research (two studies filed):**
- arXiv 2604.14210 ("Chinese Language Is Not More Efficient Than
  English in Vibe Coding"): the viral claim that Chinese prompts
  are 40% cheaper in tokens is FALSE. Controlled study across
  MiniMax-2.7, GPT-5.4-mini, GLM-5: model choice dominates —
  the 30-point model gap swamps any language effect. English
  remains the best-supported language in training data.
- ACM 3769086 (US vs China LLMs): English and Chinese are THE two
  dominant AI languages. GPT-4 Turbo leads in English; Chinese
  models (Ernie-Bot 4, Tongyi Qianwen 2) rival or exceed in
  Chinese. 88% of the world's tongues lack LLM access.

**Filed:** the AGI system's machine language is
English — code, identifiers, ledgers, math (the space's tools are
all C11 + English). The AGI's people language is the pair: English
+ Chinese, the two tongues with full AI infrastructure. The
space's choice is the only pair with complete model support.

## Step 5 — Chinese readability (the gate problem)

**The research:** Chinese readability formulas EXIST and are
published. Lin (2022, IJLT) files four features: proportion of
difficult words, number of different characters, average sentence
length, proportion of functional characters. The CCF 2017 study
derived a formula from 22 linguistic features for simplified
Chinese. The Chinese "difficulty coefficient" (Wang 2020) averages
~67 for patient education materials.

**Filed:** the plain_lang gate (RND-20) is
English-only — Flesch-Kincaid on Latin script. The DA-3 finding:
a Chinese filing cannot be gated. GOV-10 files the fix: the gate
gains a Chinese mode using the published Chinese features
(character diversity, sentence length, difficult-character
ratio). The space's law applies in both tongues.

## Step 6 — The multilingual-state costs (EU + Switzerland)

**The research:** the EU runs 24 formal tongues. There are 552
translation combinations. The bill is ~€1 billion/year — "the
price of democracy" (Commissioner Orban) — but under 1% of the
budget (~€2 per citizen). Yet the REALITY collapses: the
institutions work in 3 tongues (English, French, German),
increasingly English only. Eco: "the language of Europe is
rendering" — filed. Switzerland: 4 national tongues, no shared
one, territorial principle — works, but at the cost of
permanent translation.

**Filed:** radical multilingualism is either costly
(the EU's €1B) or constant-translation (Switzerland's). A space
with a growing registry cannot afford 24 tongues. Two standards is
the efficient middle: a working tongue and a second standard, both
fully gated, both fully filed.

## Step 7 — The verdict

**Does unifying tongues matter at all?** Yes — as *two*
standards, not one:

1. **One invented language fails** (Esperanto: no network).
2. **One imperial language breeds the ratchet** (GOV-9: the
   two-party ratchet of tongues — English-only governance would
   click one way).
3. **Two standards work** (Singapore's proven pair; the EU's
   collapse-to-3 shows the floor; the space's floor is 2).
4. **English + Chinese is the strongest pair on Earth.** They are
   the two dominant AI tongues (full model support). They are
   the two largest speaker populations (English ~1.5B, Chinese
   ~1.1B). One is the working tongue (code, math, ledgers). The
   other is a second standard with depth (history, identity, the
   most spoken L1). The island converged on exactly this pair.
5. **The gate must speak both** — GOV-10 files the Two-Tongue
   Doctrine and extends plain_lang with a Chinese mode.

The User's choice is not preference alone; it is the
correct decision, and the math backs it. Filed.
