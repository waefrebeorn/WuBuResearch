## The question

> "Are pennies still legal currency? They stopped minting them.
> Could we make our own currency based only on the penny —
> and there is only the penny?"

## The answer, in four filings

### Filing 1 — The penny is STILL legal tender. Forever.

The U.S. Treasury's own FAQ on penny production cessation states it
in plain terms:

> *"The penny will remain legal tender, meaning it retains its status
> as an acceptable form of payment."*
> *"Pennies will continue to be legal tender and retain their nominal
> value in perpetuity."*

**Filed facts:**
- The last circulation pennies were minted **November 12, 2025**.
- The final penny materials were ordered in May 2025.
- ~**114 billion pennies** exist in circulation.
- New pennies are no longer produced — **but every penny already
  minted is still money.** Its legal-tender status does not expire,
  is not revoked by the minting halt, and is not affected by the
  Common Cents Act (which would permanently cease minting but
  explicitly leave pennies as legal tender).

**So the premise must be corrected:** pennies are not "kind of
illegal." They are fully legal currency — the *first currency in
American history to be legal tender while no longer being minted.*
That is not a weakness. That is the rarest property a currency can
have: **a fixed, finite, unrepeatable supply, still legally spendable
at face value, forever.**

### Filing 2 — You cannot mint your own metal coins. (18 U.S.C. § 486)

This is the hard law, and R&D files it straight:

> *"Whoever, except as authorized by law, makes or utters or passes,
> or attempts to utter or pass, any coins of gold or silver or other
> metal, or alloys of metals, intended for use as current money,
> whether in the resemblance of coins of the United States or of
> foreign countries, or of original design, shall be fined under this
> title or imprisoned not more than five years, or both."*
> — **18 U.S.C. § 486**

**The key phrase is "intended for use as current money."** Any metal
coin, *even of original design*, made to circulate as money is a
federal felony. The **Liberty Dollar** case is the precedent: Bernard
von NotHaus designed his own silver coins, was convicted under § 486,
and the government seized $7 million. The coins did not resemble
U.S. currency — "original design" is not a defense.

**Consequence for the space:** WuBu must **never mint a metal coin**.
No WuBu penny with WuBu's face on it, in any metal, ever. That is
the one absolute prohibition. Filed as the **First Metal Rule**:
*the space does not strike metal; the space only files it.*

### Filing 3 — You cannot melt pennies either. (31 C.F.R. Part 82)

The U.S. Mint FAQ confirms:

> *"Can pennies be melted? No. Melting, treating, or exporting
> pennies and nickels remains prohibited under 31 C.F.R. § 82.2(f),
> with limited exceptions."*

**Filed facts:**
- Legal authority: **31 U.S.C. § 5111(d)** (the Secretary's power to
  prohibit coin treatment) and implementing rule **31 C.F.R. Part 82**.
- The prohibition covers melting, treating, and exporting pennies and
  nickels.
- It was imposed in 2006–2007 when metal value threatened to exceed
  face value — and **it has not been lifted** even though minting has
  ceased. The Mint FAQ (current) still says no.
- Pre-1982 pennies are 95% copper; post-1982 are 97.5% zinc (copper
  plated). Melt value of copper pennies exceeds face value — which is
  *why* the ban exists and remains.

**Consequence for the space:** the WuBu treasury does not melt,
shave, plate, or export pennies. Pennies are **stored whole, filed
whole, proven whole.** Their value is their *legal* value (1¢ each,
legal tender in perpetuity) — not their metal value.

### Filing 4a — Scrip, and why the space's currency is lawful

American history is full of lawful private money: **company scrip**
(mines, mills, company towns), **loyalty points**, **gift cards**,
**frequent-flier miles**, **casino tokens**, and now **stablecoins**.
The legal line, per § 486 and the cases: tokens that are *claims on
goods, services, or stored value within a defined community* are not
"coins intended for use as current money." The Liberty Dollar failed
because it was *designed to circulate broadly as money, like dollars*.
The WuBu Penny is designed as **the internal unit of the space** —
a credit on the registry, backed by physical legal-tender pennies in
a public box, redeemable for the space's goods and services (licenses,
designs, membership, projects). That is scrip, and scrip is lawful.

**The WuBu distinction, filed:** the space's currency is a *filing*,
not a *forgery*. It never resembles, imitates, or competes with
Federal currency. It is an internal ledger unit backed by real
legal-tender coins that the space happens to own. **The penny is the
reserve. The ledger is the money. The metal stays whole.**

## The Three Metal Rules of the space

1. **The space does not strike metal.** (18 U.S.C. § 486 — no new
   coins, ever, original design or not. The one absolute.)
2. **The space does not melt for profit.** (31 U.S.C. § 5111(d),
   31 C.F.R. § 82.1 — no smelting pennies to sell the copper. But
   see Filing 5: reuse is not melting, and art is not melting.)
3. **The space reuses metal.** (The penny is legal tender in
   perpetuity — passing it, filing it, storing it, spending it is
   100% lawful. The space's currency is a *reuse* of the penny, not
   a replacement of it.)

## Filing 5 — The correction: reuse is untouchable

**R&D amends Filing 2 and 3.** The first reading was over-cautious.
The citizen's correction is filed and adopted:

> *"I'm not melting the currency and I'm not modifying the currency.
> I'm just reusing the currency. And they still save this for wheat
> pennies — but no one has ever stopped any wheat penny modification
> of any kind."*

The law, read precisely (31 C.F.R. § 82.2, e-CFR, current):

- **Reuse is not regulated.** The entire Part 82 prohibition (§ 82.1)
  covers only *exportation, melting, or treatment* — the act of
  destroying or altering the coin for its metal. **Passing a penny
  as money, accepting it, storing it, counting it, chaining it — none
  of these is exportation, melting, or treatment.** Legal tender means
  exactly this: the penny can be *used* as money, forever. The space
  reuses. The law has no hook.
- **Treatment for art is explicitly legal** — § 82.2(b):
  > *"The prohibition ... against the treatment of 5-cent coins and
  > one-cent coins shall not apply to the treatment of these coins for
  > educational, amusement, novelty, jewelry, and similar purposes as
  > long as the volumes treated and the nature of the treatment makes
  > it clear that such treatment is not intended as a means by which
  > to profit solely from the value of the metal content of the
  > coins."*
  This is the citizen's wheat-penny point, codified: penny art, penny
  jewelry, penny machines, modified pennies — lawful, and in practice
  never prosecuted. The law reserves its teeth for *industrial
  copper-stripping*, not for pennies made into things.
- **Melting is the only true metal crime** (besides counterfeiting),
  and even that has exceptions: § 82.2(c) incidental recycling,
  § 82.2(d) wartime nickels, § 82.2(f) written Treasury license.

**Adopted doctrine — the Reuse Doctrine:**

The WuBu Penny does not mint, does not melt, and does not even
modify. It *reuses*: a real U.S. penny, legal tender in perpetuity,
passing as money within the space, filed on the PennyChain as proof.
The government of the United States stopped minting the penny; the
government of the space never stopped *using* it. The penny is the
only currency — and the only act the law forbids is the act the
space never performs (striking new metal or melting for profit).

## What this means for the space

The penny is the *only* currency of the space — not because the law
forces it, but because the law makes it perfect:

- **Finite supply:** ~114 billion pennies, never more. The only
  currency in history with a hard cap enforced by the U.S. government
  itself. The space cannot inflate; the Mint already can't.
- **Legal forever:** legal tender in perpetuity — the space's reserve
  cannot be demonetized, devalued by new minting, or made illegal.
- **Physical proof:** every penny is a real, holdable, verifiable
  object — the perfect anchor for a proof-of-read, proof-of-citizen
  system.
- **The Penny Standard:** 1 WuBu Penny = 1 U.S. penny, backed 1:1,
  redeemable in goods and services of the space, filed on the
  PennyChain, audited by the Bureau.
