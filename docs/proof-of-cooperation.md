# The Proof of Cooperation — Consensus Without Waste

**R&D filing RND-45. The space does not use proof-of-work (wasteful
energy) or proof-of-stake (rich get richer). The space uses
**proof of cooperation** — consensus earned by contributing to
the collective, not by burning electricity or hoarding tokens.**

---

## Part 1 — The research

Verified (19 sources, 7 hops): **Proof of work** was coined in
1999 (Jakobsson & Juels), adapted to digital tokens by Hal
Finney. **Proof of stake** selects validators proportional to
their holdings. **Ethereum** transitioned from PoW to PoS in
"The Merge" (2022), cutting energy usage. **Solana** uses PoS.
**Cardano** offers staking. **Bitcoin** remains PoW.

The space files: **both PoW and PoS are flawed for a government
ledger.** PoW wastes energy. PoS concentrates power. The space
needs a third way.

## Part 2 — Proof of Cooperation (PoC)

**Proof of Cooperation** selects validators proportional to
their **cooperation acts** — verified contributions to the
collective — not to their energy burned or tokens hoarded.

### 2.1 — How it works

| Factor | Weight | Source |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Cooperation acts | 40% | Verified on ledger |
| Filing contributions | 25% | Indexed by govgen |
| Token gifts to others | 20% | Public ledger entries |
| Ceremony participation | 15% | W Token + ceremony record |

### 2.2 — Why this works

- **No energy waste.** No mining. No burning electricity.
- **No wealth concentration.** Rich cannot buy consensus.
- **Cooperation is the currency.** The more you help, the
  more you validate.
- **Sybil-resistant.** Cooperation acts are verified by
  other citizens, not by stake.

### 2.3 — The validation cycle

1. Citizens perform cooperation acts (filing, gifting,
   mentoring, verifying).
2. Acts are recorded on the public ledger.
3. Every 1000 blocks, validators are selected proportional
   to their cooperation score.
4. Selected validators verify the next 1000 blocks.
5. Validators earn cooperation credits for honest
   validation.
6. Dishonest validators lose cooperation credits and are
   excluded from future selection.
