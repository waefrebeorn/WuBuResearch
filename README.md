## What the R&D Department is

WuBuResearch is the **research and development department** of WuBuGov —
solely and entirely dedicated to:

1. **Organizing the information** — every finding, every mechanism, every
   scrap of intelligence from the whole space, filed, indexed, and made
   usable.
2. **Researching the best** — we study the best governments, the best
   cryptographic systems, the best scoring mechanisms, the best
   institutions — and we take from all of them. Everything that works
   gets filed. Everything that fails gets filed too (that is how you
   know).
3. **Inventing the new** — new and different cryptography, new scoring
   math, new mechanisms that no government has ever built, because the
   space is not Earth and Earth's tools are only the starting point.

## How the R&D Department works with the Bureau

The division of labor is precise:

| Who | Role |
|-----|------|
| **The Bureau** (WuBuIntel) | The guys on the ground. Gathering information, watching, verifying, and **tracking the pennies** — the physical proof of the whole space. |
| **R&D** (WuBuResearch) | The guys at the desk. Organizing everything the Bureau brings in, researching the best mechanisms, inventing the cryptography the space runs on. |

The loop is a loop:

```
Bureau gathers (ground truth, penny counts)
   ↓
R&D organizes (filing, indexing, dedup)
   ↓
R&D researches (best governments, best crypto, best math)
   ↓
R&D invents (new mechanisms, WuBu cryptography)
   ↓
Bureau deploys (watchdog the new system, track the pennies it produces)
   ↓
(loop forever — the space never stops learning)
```

**Penny tracking** is the Bureau's ground job. **Penny ledger math** —
the hash chains, the keyless signatures, the anti-forgery — is R&D's
desk job. One counts, the other proves.

## The R&D method

Every research item follows the same five steps:

1. **Take from the best.** Find the government, system, or paper that
   does it best. Study it. Cite it. (We take from all the best
   governments — that is the mandate.)
2. **File the truth.** What works, what fails, what the numbers say.
3. **Find the spine.** The part that a space-faring cooperative needs.
4. **Rebuild it WuBu.** Adapted to the license, the penny, the ledger,
   the crew.
5. **Invent beyond it.** Where Earth's tools stop, the space invents
   the next step. New and different. Ours.

## The research log

Every finding is dated and filed in `docs/research-log.md`. The log is
append-only. The log is public. A research department that hides its
findings is not research — it is a cult. The R&D Department files.
